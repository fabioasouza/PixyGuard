PixyGuard Privacy Notice — 0.5.9-unified

PixyGuard is designed to operate locally on your Mac.

PixyGuard does NOT intentionally:
- record or save camera video frames;
- record audio;
- record keyboard input or keystrokes;
- upload camera content;
- send analytics or telemetry;
- create a user account.

PixyGuard reads local camera-device metadata such as whether the EMEET PIXY is connected or currently in use by another application. It also reads the local macOS session state (for example, locked/unlocked) and sends local HID control commands to the camera.

macOS may classify the low-level HID permission under Input Monitoring. PixyGuard requests this permission to control the PIXY HID interface, not to inspect keyboard content.

Local diagnostic logs may contain timestamps and camera/control status. They remain on the Mac unless the user chooses to share them.
